# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — Initial Release

- **Quest Tracker Typography:** Customize font family, outline (*None*, *Outline*, *Thick Outline*, *Monochrome*, *Outline + Monochrome*), drop shadow, and independent sizes (`8–32 px`) for Section Headers, Quest Titles, and Objective Lines.
- **Built-in & SharedMedia Support:** Ships with 5 default client fonts (*Friz Quadrata*, *Arial Narrow*, *Morpheus*, *Skurri*, *Friends*) and automatically detects extra fonts, backgrounds, and borders from `LibSharedMedia-3.0`.
- **Textured Backgrounds & Borders:** Includes 7 background textures (*Dialog Dark*, *Solid Flat*, *Tooltip*, *Dialog Classic*, *Parchment*, *Marble*, *Rock*), 6 borders (*Tooltip*, *None*, *Thin 1px*, *Dialog*, *Gold*, *Achievement Wood*), 7 color tint presets, opacity (`0–100%`), padding (`0–24 px`), and automatic tight fit around tracked quests.
- **Overflow Scroll Bar:** Automatically attaches a draggable, tint-matched vertical scroll bar whenever tracked quests exceed the configurable **Max Quest Window Height** (`20–90%` of screen height).
- **Dual Tracker Support:** Works out of the box with both **Questie Tracker** and **Blizzard's Objective Tracker** on **WoW Forever (`1.60.1`)** and **Retail Midnight (`12.1.5+`)**.
- **Live Config & Native Settings:** Includes a draggable tabbed live-preview window (`/qfont`), full Blizzard **Options → AddOns → qfont** integration, Minimap Addon Compartment support, and complete slash commands.
